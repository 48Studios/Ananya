The module is split into two cooperating layers: `app/` exposes a FastAPI surface (`main.py`, `services/*`) that loads the active model from `models/registry/<version>/category_classifier.pkl` via `model_registry.py`, while `training/` provides the offline pipeline (collectors, processors, trainers, TUI) that produces those artifacts. The `pipeline/` scripts are thin CLI wrappers around `training/` modules for build/evaluate/train/deploy workflows, and both layers share configuration via `app/config.py` and `training/config/settings.py`. Models are versioned under `models/registry/` with an `active_deployment.json` pointer that the runtime reads to pick the live artifact, and datasets under `data/datasets/` plus `training/datasets/training/` provide deterministic inputs for training and benchmarks. Tests in `tests/` exercise both the HTTP endpoints and the training pipeline control plane against local fixtures.
