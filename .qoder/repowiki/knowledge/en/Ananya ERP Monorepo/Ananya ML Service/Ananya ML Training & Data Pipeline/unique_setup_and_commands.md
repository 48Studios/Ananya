The `ananya-ml` CLI under `apps/ml/training/cli.py` is the unified entry point: `collect` (autonomous crawling with policy/rate limits), `data-export|validate|normalize|dedupe|inspect|coverage|plan|reprocess-category`, `dataset-build|split`, `train` (with `--legacy` to fall back to `apps/ml/pipeline/run_training.py`), `evaluate`, and `experiment`. Global flags `-q/--quiet`, `-v/--verbose`, and `--tui/--no-tui` control progress output and the Rich dashboard across every subcommand.
