This module is a flat artifact layout rather than executable code: `raw/` holds the immutable crawl inputs (PDFs, HTML, catalogs, fixtures) and versioned `training/dataset-crawl-*` splits consumed by downstream processors, while `cleaned/`, `normalized/`, and `evaluation/` are reserved staging directories where those processors write their outputs. The `splitter.py` script at the module root is the only code, bridging the raw crawl artifacts into the processed stages used by the rest of the training pipeline.
